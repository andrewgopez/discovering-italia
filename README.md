# Discovering Italia

Discovering Italia is a cultural and educational website project that explores Italy as a whole and its 20 diverse regions through geography, history, landmarks, cuisine, languages, traditions, art, music, and regional identities. The website takes visitors on a journey across Italy, highlighting the unique characteristics and heritage that make each region distinct while also showcasing Italy’s shared national identity.

From ancient Roman landmarks and Renaissance masterpieces to regional dishes, local traditions, and famous cities, Discovering Italia aims to present the beauty, diversity, and history of Italy in an engaging and accessible way.

## Inspiration

During the Winter 2025 semester at John Abbott College (CEGEP), I took a course called Special Topics in Italian Culture. I greatly enjoyed exploring Italian history, culture, and regional identity through the course, and this experience inspired me to create a website dedicated to showcasing Italy’s cultural diversity.

## Features

- Explore all 20 Italian regions through dedicated regional pages
- Overview of Italy’s history, geography, culture, and national identity
- Regional navigation system for easy exploration
- Information on languages, food, art, music, landmarks, and traditions
- Landmark, city, and food cards with images
- Responsive design for desktop, tablet, and mobile devices
- Dark mode and back-to-top functionality
- Clean museum-inspired design
